# Echo Grid Community — Membership Terms (draft)

Version: {version} · Effective: {effectiveDate}

## A. Eligibility and participation
A1. Echo Grid Community is separate from the loyalty programs of individual businesses. Joining a business's loyalty program does not make you a Community member.
A2. Community membership is voluntary. You become a member only when you expressly agree to these terms to join. The same applies each time you rejoin after leaving and each time you restore a membership that was set inactive.
A3. To join, rejoin or restore, your Echo Grid account must be active and have a verified phone number, and must not be suspended or banned. An account that is scheduled for deletion cannot join, rejoin or restore until the deletion request is cancelled.
A4. During a pilot, Community may be offered only to selected accounts. Being selected does not make you a member; you must still agree to these terms.

## B. Responsibilities and conduct
B1. You agree to provide accurate information and to keep your account secure.
B2. You agree not to harass others, commit fraud or abuse, or manipulate Community Points.
B3. Echo Grid may moderate Community content and may limit or remove features where these terms are breached.

## C. Suspension, termination and disputes
C1. Echo Grid may suspend or end a membership for a breach of these terms and will give reasons where it reasonably can.
C2. A suspended membership cannot be reactivated by you. If Echo Grid lifts a suspension, your membership becomes inactive, not active. To become active again you must expressly agree to these terms again.
C3. If you disagree with a decision, you may contact Echo Grid support to ask for a review. [[LEGAL-DECISION D1: dispute procedure, timelines and escalation]]

## D. Community Points
D1. Community Points have no monetary value. They cannot be redeemed for cash, cannot be transferred between customers, and are not a financial asset.
D2. Community Points are separate from the points of any business loyalty program.
D3. Community Points are recorded in an audit ledger. Adjustments and reversals are recorded as separate entries. Echo Grid may correct errors; no balance may be changed without a recorded entry.
D4. If you leave Community, your Community Points account is closed. [[LEGAL-DECISION D2: what happens to the balance after leaving, and whether it is restored on rejoin]]

## E. Privacy, consent evidence, leaving and account deletion
E1. When you agree to these terms, Echo Grid records evidence of your agreement: the version, the language shown, and the time. This evidence cannot be edited. [[LEGAL-DECISION D3: retention period for consent evidence]]
E2. You may leave Community at any time. Leaving ends your participation. It does not delete your account.
E3. Asking to delete your account data is a separate request with a 30-day period in which you can restore the account. While a deletion request is pending you cannot join, rejoin or restore Community, but you can still leave. If you cancel the deletion request, you may join again by agreeing to these terms again.
E4. Some records must be kept even after you leave or your data is deleted, for example records required by law or under a legal hold. [[LEGAL-DECISION D4: retained categories, durations and legal-hold procedure]]

## F. Changes and versions
F1. Each version of these terms has a unique identifier and an effective date. Earlier versions remain on record.
F2. If the terms change in a material way, you must agree to the new version before you can join, rejoin or restore.
F3. [[LEGAL-DECISION D5: treatment of existing members when terms change]]

## G. Consent statements shown to you
G1. Join: "I expressly agree to join Echo Grid Community under these terms, version {version}."
G2. Rejoin: "I expressly agree to rejoin Echo Grid Community under these terms, version {version}."
G3. Restore: "I expressly agree to restore my Echo Grid Community membership under these terms, version {version}."
G4. Each statement is shown unchecked. Nothing is agreed unless you actively choose it.
