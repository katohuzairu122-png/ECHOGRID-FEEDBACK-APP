## A. Eligibility

Community membership is for customers using their own authenticated Echo Grid
account. Joining requires an active eligible account, a phone number whose
ownership is verified through Echo Grid's existing account process, and explicit
acceptance of the then-approved membership terms. Typing a number or returning
from verification does not prove ownership or create membership.

A suspended Community membership cannot be reactivated by its customer. Account
restrictions and existing platform moderation requirements continue to apply.
A verified phone does not grant business, staff, publishing or moderator powers.

## B. Membership

Membership is voluntary. We propose that joining carries no Community membership
fee. One customer account has at most one Community membership and one separate
Community Points account. Your customer account, business-specific loyalty
memberships and Community membership are different relationships.

Opening Community, reading information, signing in, scanning a QR code, completing
phone verification or using business loyalty does not enroll you. Join and Rejoin
require separate deliberate actions after the applicable terms are presented.
There is no automatic renewal, payment instruction or financial activation in
this proposal.

[Review D1: approve the free-membership proposition and exact wording. This draft
does not make existing business subscriptions or other features free.]

## C. Participation and conduct

Membership does not enable every Community capability. Only features actually
available and independently authorized for your account may be used. Feed access
continues to require a valid active customer identity, authoritative verified
phone, enabled Feed and permitted published content. Membership does not override
content visibility, expiry, audience, moderation or tenant restrictions.

Do not impersonate others, circumvent account or verification controls, submit
fraudulent eligibility evidence, exploit duplicate awards, or use available
features for unlawful or abusive purposes. Official publishing remains controlled
by authorized platform roles and the existing independent-review process.
Customer posting, reactions, comments, messaging, advertising and future features
are not granted by accepting these membership terms. Any future contribution
rules require separate approval and presentation before that capability opens.

[Review: align conduct wording with the existing `/terms` acceptable-use language.
Do not invent new enforcement powers or represent reserved features as available.]

## D. Community Points

Community Points are separate from each business's loyalty points. Businesses'
loyalty balances do not become a pooled Community balance. Membership itself does
not guarantee an award, a point amount or a benefit. Only qualifying evidence and
the existing approved server-side Community Points rules can generate awards.
An explicit eligible join may release previously pending awards under those rules;
repeated requests must not issue the same award twice.

These terms promise no monetary value, cash redemption, automatic transferability,
exchange rate, payout or right to spend Community Points at a business. They do
not establish new earning, expiry, reversal, transfer or redemption rules. Existing
approved Community Points policies remain authoritative.

[Review: confirm the customer-facing explanation of the actual available benefits.
Do not insert proposed financial activation or a forfeiture rule into this draft.]

## E. Privacy and acceptance records

Echo Grid needs membership status and minimal acceptance evidence to operate this
relationship and protect its integrity. The proposed evidence identifies your
customer account, the exact terms version and content, the server-recorded time,
your explicit Join/Rejoin action and the associated membership transition. It is
separate from consent for advertising, marketing, contact sharing, surveys or
business loyalty. Acceptance is not permission for unrelated processing.

The applicable Privacy Policy must be made available alongside the approved terms.
The existing repository provides `/privacy`; that page's existence is not approval
of this new membership notice or its legal basis. The technical proposal minimizes
new evidence: it excludes passwords, tokens, phone numbers, emails, IP addresses,
user-agent strings and raw request bodies from Community acceptance events.

Leaving does not itself delete your global account or every historical record.
Privacy requests, legal holds, evidence retention and identity anonymization must
follow separately approved policies and the existing privacy architecture.

## F. Leaving

You may request to leave through the existing confirmed Leave control, subject to
the existing membership-state rules. Leaving changes your Community membership
to left and closes its Community Points account. Under the current implementation,
the balance and accounting history are retained; leaving does not award, transfer,
redeem or erase points. This draft introduces no forfeiture or restoration promise.

Leaving Community does not delete your customer account, end business-specific
loyalty memberships, remove business loyalty balances or cancel business rewards.
Feed eligibility is a separate rule; leaving does not create or remove permission
beyond what that existing rule specifies. Suspended members cannot override the
platform suspension using the self-service Leave action.

[Review: confirm the explanation of access while left and how existing pending
awards are treated. No new appeal or exception to suspension is established.]

## G. Rejoining

Rejoining requires a valid current customer session, a fresh server eligibility
check, the currently effective approved terms and a new explicit acceptance.
Earlier acceptance does not automatically authorize a new membership transition.
Rejoining preserves the original customer, membership, points account and history;
it must not create duplicate accounts or duplicate awards.

A prior suspension or separately revoked privilege is not removed by phone
verification, acceptance or Rejoin. Reverification through SMS is not required
merely because an already-authoritative verified phone exists, unless a separate
approved account-security rule requires step-up.

## H. Updates to these terms

An approved publication must identify its exact version, immutable content and
effective date, and make the applicable localized wording accessible. A version
identifier must not be reused for changed content. A proposed or future-effective
version cannot silently replace the currently effective one.

Material changes may require renewed explicit acceptance only under an approved
change/reacceptance policy. Continued use, reading, verification or a preselected
checkbox is not a substitute for a required affirmative action. Refusing a new
version must not silently erase points, enroll a customer or change unrelated
business loyalty rights.

[Review D6: define materiality, notice, the scope and timing of renewed acceptance,

## I. Enforcement, questions and disputes

Existing account restrictions and Community moderation controls remain applicable.
Membership does not add moderator authority or remove the protections around
published content. Suspension and restoration follow the existing authorized
platform process; customers cannot restore their own suspended membership.

Questions can use Echo Grid's existing support interface at `/support`. That link
is not a newly promised appeal service or resolution deadline. Appeals are offered
only where an existing approved policy supports them.
