## Point evidence and invalidation

Resolve trusted local business, permission, customer/subscription/item lifecycle, account, mode, and requested invoice before acquisition. Require the returned scope to match the independently established expectation. Keep provenance and monetary fields with their units and meanings. A point observation cannot supply economic eligibility or settlement by itself.

Record server request and receipt timestamps, clock basis and uncertainty, provider event time when present, source and method versions, binding/assertion revision, config/policy revision, credential/attestation revision, incident head and immutable evidence reference. Distinguish acquisition timing from provider effective time. There is zero operational reliance beyond observation. No provider-backed maximum age or clock-skew limit is established. Unknown clock uncertainty, missing scope, expired evidence, unavailable revision or conflict denies reliance.

At evaluation and before any separately authorized operation, recheck business permission and ownership, scoped account/mode and customer/subscription lifecycle, exact invoice identity and state, credential/attestation and configuration/policy heads, binding supersession and incident restrictions. Read the current historical assessment head and challenges. Reconcile disputes and reversals against independent scoped provider evidence and local recovery records. A previous assertion can remain audit evidence after revocation. A changed local revision causes rollback and reevaluation under the same operation identity. The common transaction fence and restricted role boundary still require design approval and verification.

## Race dispositions

| Scenario | Required disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Draft finalizes between retrieval and adjustment | Immediate reread can detect a completed transition. It cannot prevent a later one. Deny mutable-state-dependent execution without qualified provider admission and any required precondition. Keep the exact target. |
| Same invoice ID, changed state, lines or amount | Compare full scoped state and economic inputs. If the needed invariant cannot be checked atomically at provider admission, deny reliance. |
| Provider operation succeeded, response lost | Keep canonical request, original key and outstanding obligation. Independently read back and reconcile exact instrument identity, invoice, reservation, amount, currency and complete lines. |
| Duplicate retry with unknown result | Hold unresolved. A cached 500 or potentially pruned key cannot establish absence or permit a new operation. |
| Customer/subscription binding changes | Deny on new mismatch or revision. Preserve the original attempt and evidence for scoped reconciliation. |
| Local source revision changes before commit | Future local transaction must serialize/recheck relevant heads and abort on mismatch. This cannot order external Stripe state changes. |
| Benefit confirmed, settlement incomplete | Retain obligation and deny final consumption. Independent settlement and separately authorized ledger transaction remain required. |

## Decision and evidence

Approve the point-evidence profile only after confirming the exact source principal, supported direct or Connect inventory, business binding proof and supported subscription interpretation. Keep operational validity unset. Verify runtime behavior under the pin, including finalization contention and independent effect retrieval, before any write-dependent reliance. Resolve clock uncertainty and any commit-spanning guarantee separately. A local fence, repeated GET or provider idempotency cannot stand in for that guarantee. No substitute invoice, replacement idempotency key, pending-item fallback or repeated application is admissible.
