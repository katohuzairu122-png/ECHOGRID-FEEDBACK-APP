## Freshness and invalidation

Record trusted local request start and receipt times, clock basis, method/version, request correlation, account/mode and immutable manifest. Keep provider event creation and effective times distinct from acquisition and local commit. The local bracket records acquisition timing. Stripe documentation reviewed here establishes no maximum age or clock-skew bound for operational reliance. Give current invoice state zero reliance beyond the observation. Missing approved validity bounds, unverifiable clock, unavailable source, conflicting evidence or unknown context deny new reliance. Signature freshness and event retention do not extend invoice validity.

At evaluation and immediately before any future authorized operation, re-read the exact invoice and compare scope and lifecycle. Verify subscription cancellation or replacement using scoped provider observations and current local lifecycle evidence. Resolve business ownership and permission from trusted local records. Recheck current binding, configuration, credential inventory, incident and historical assessment heads. An authentication failure or unavailable credential/incident status denies reliance. New contrary observations supersede reliance while retaining original audit evidence. Resolve reversed or disputed effects through scoped provider reconciliation and local dispute and recovery records. Delayed events cannot clear a deny. An expired assertion can be read for audit. New reliance requires new evidence under the original operation identity.

## Assurance and residual race

1. Observation establishes the returned state in that authenticated context at acquisition. Retain it with provenance.
2. Immediate revalidation narrows the race. Local revision fencing orders local changes only. The reviewed provider contract supplies no atomic observation-to-local-commit guarantee. Finalization, line changes and external changes can occur after a successful read. If the operation depends on unchanged state and lacks a documented provider precondition, deny it. Require the specific invoice ID and approved account/mode. Never use a pending-item fallback or replacement invoice.
3. If a future separately authorized operation has already been attempted, retain its exact canonical request and stable key. Independently retrieve the original instrument and complete invoice lines, matching its immutable reference, original reservation, invoice, amount, currency and scope. Preserve unknown, conflicting or incomplete results as unresolved. A timeout, 500, finalization or elapsed key retention cannot justify a fresh attempt or automatic compensation. Even a confirmed effect requires independent settlement evidence and the separate authorized consumption transaction.

Unsupported cases include unverified Connect/realm context, ambiguous subscription lineage, missing item attribution, incomplete pagination, historical gaps and a mutable-state acceptance condition without a provider guarantee. No source method here qualifies for an operational validity window. Proposed owner decision is to approve the scoped read only as point evidence and require version-specific endpoint and race evidence before admitting any write-dependent reliance.
