## Proposed immutable binding identity

Recommend an immutable binding reference as the interface between a private trusted resolver and a future append validator. This is a logical contract, not approval of a table layout, a migration number or a new key. The final schema choice remains subject to architecture review.

The record must retain a versioned typed identity tuple and compare its full fields on every lookup/replay; a digest may index the tuple but cannot substitute for equality or authentication:

1. Provider namespace and attested provider isolation realm, origin/principal account, approved direct/connected context and operating mode. Reuse the applicable HETA scoped interpretation; do not substitute deployment names, secret prefixes or a null-account default. Unsupported contexts remain unresolved. An invoice reference is not a provider Event ID and must not be placed into an Event identity tuple.
2. ECHO GRID business UUID and immutable business-ownership/binding revision; Stripe customer, subscription and verified lifecycle references. Branch scope must derive from the authenticated business and existing permissions rather than create another customer identity or ledger.
3. Invoice reference, currency, supported policy versions, original persisted reservation and invoice-intent references, requested finite effective window and immutable source evidence references. A batch retains the exact original reservation set; no alternate invoice, subscription or account may be substituted on retry.
4. Binding format/rule version, immutable assertion ID/revision, expected predecessor, producer/service identity and auditable decision provenance. Current business permission is evaluated separately and cannot be preserved forever by an old binding.

Same provider identifier strings in distinct realms/accounts/modes are distinct identities. Customer reuse, multiple subscriptions or subscription replacement cannot be resolved by latest arrival, metadata, current mirror or a business-wide default. Require proof of the unique applicable lifecycle for the requested scope; ambiguity is a denial. Preserve global customer identity and business-isolated relationships; billing provenance does not create customer loyalty membership or consent.

Existing allocation uniqueness `(business_id, invoice_ref)` does not encode all these fields. Until a separately reviewed immutable link or scoped key design guarantees no aliasing, a second account/mode binding with the same allocation key must reject. No key is widened and no allocation is rebound here.

## Evidence manifest and production boundary

The proposed producer is a private authenticated billing/evidence workload with an independently approved inventory and narrowly scoped source access. It must prove which service produced the assertion and which exact source/method/config versions support each claim. An actor string provided by a caller is insufficient. Public/customer/staff input may request a permitted action, but may not select trusted principal, realm, tenant binding, status, approval or provider context.

Each immutable assertion requires a manifest containing:

| Claim group                          | Evidence required                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Provider context                     | Independently verified account/realm/mode/acquisition context and immutable approved inventory/attestation references; endpoint provenance is retained separately from invoice ownership.                                                                  |
| Business ownership                   | Tenant-checked customer/subscription lifecycle proof and exact binding revision; no metadata-only or current-mirror-only assignment.                                                                                                                       |
| Invoice identity and supported state | Independently acquired invoice identity/customer/subscription/currency in the verified context, source/API interpretation version and checked-at reference; separate the approved draft application question from paid/settled final-consumption evidence. |
| Historical requirement               | HETA certificate/assessment identity, explicit finite coverage scope, rule/mapping/binding revisions, manifest and current challenge/incident state where that requirement applies. Logical certified status is still non-activating.                      |
| Economics input                      | Approved eligible-base/policy evidence and exact numeric values used by the existing calculator; no tax/settlement default or caller-computed amount gains authority.                                                                                      |
| Integrity and provenance             | Original observation references/digests, acquisition type, source principal/context, committed record reference and verifier version. A hash proves content integrity only; API retrieval cannot manufacture original webhook provenance.                  |
| Validity and lifecycle               | Finite validity interval, observed/retrieved/recorded times, knowledge/revision cutoff, lineage, revocation/suspension/compromise and independently supported recovery references.                                                                         |

Do not store credentials, secret-derived values, provider authorization headers, raw customer/payment payloads or contact information in the binding/audit. Use existing protected evidence references and minimal redacted identifiers/digests. A new evidence producer, credential profile, access scope, storage retention rule or read-only provider probe requires its own reviewed execution authority; this proposal performs no network verification or credential access.

## Time, coverage and freshness

Keep provider effective time, observation/retrieval time, assertion recorded time, evaluation time, knowledge cutoff and Split 06 fulfillment time distinct. Use finite UTC half-open windows `[from,to)` with `from < to`; at exactly `to` an assertion provides no new reliance. Event creation, receipt order, billing-period start or a fresh current invoice cannot infer a historical state boundary or fill a coverage gap.

For a required historical query, identity and the complete nonoverlapping partition must be proved before selecting `from <= instant < to`. HETA's certified/incomplete/conflicted/unproven logical states and defect precedence remain unchanged. Certified covers only its explicit bounded scope/revision; it never sets `historicalTierVerified`, `issuanceAuthorized` or `redemptionAuthorized` true through this proposal. Incomplete, conflicted, unsupported or unproven required evidence denies dependent use, even when one local candidate matches.

Freshness must be an owner-approved method profile with explicit validity bounds, clock basis/skew treatment, revocation source and evidence-acquisition requirements. No arbitrary TTL, infinite validity, stale-while-revalidate or extension of a prior assertion is selected here. Missing approved method/freshness policy means no usable context. A cached decision expires at the earliest applicable source/assertion/config validity boundary and is also bound to current revisions; TTL alone is insufficient invalidation.

Provider data can change outside a local transaction. A local lock cannot make the provider state immutable or prove absence of omitted changes. The approved method must state exactly which facts are immutable, which require renewed observation and what guarantee supports reliance across the acquisition-to-commit interval. If that guarantee cannot be established, dependent append remains denied; do not invent a provider lock, current-state continuity or a freshness duration.

## Proposed resolver result and safe use

The conceptual private resolver accepts an authenticated server context, the requested complete scope and an explicit evaluation/knowledge context. Public payloads do not supply a trusted context. It must authorize the business before revealing whether a binding exists and return no private cross-business relationship or provider payload.

Proposed outcomes are `usable_context`, `denied` and `not_found`, distinct from HETA coverage states. A usable result contains the immutable binding/assertion references, exact scope, manifest digest, interpretation versions, validity bounds, current config/incident/head revision references and minimum source expiry. Every outcome retains `providerVerified=false` and `consumptionAuthorized=false`. `usable_context` is a proposed identity/provenance result, not an append grant, invoice benefit, provider-write permission or financial authorization. No such resolver is implemented today.

Deny for unknown scope, unsupported context/version, missing provenance, ambiguous ownership/lifecycle, stale/expired source, interval gap/overlap, incomplete pagination/capture, required history unproven, revoked/compromised/suspended evidence, conflicting manifest, missing current incident view or mismatched expected revision. Log redacted internal reason codes through an authorized path; caller responses must not disclose another tenant's existence or evidence.

For future nonfinancial append use, the database boundary must verify the persisted binding/provenance and the caller's permitted operation; it cannot merely accept a resolver's JSON or an in-memory branded type. Authorization context and its persistence/verification mechanism remain a required implementation contract. A shared application database role does not by itself prove an end user's business permission.

## Revocation, lineage and commit races

Retain immutable assertions and append lifecycle revisions with exact predecessor, scope, reason, authorized actor, recorded/effective times and supporting evidence. Reuse the applicable HETA monotonic/expected-head rules; do not create a competing mutable binding-history source. A derived current-head index may be rebuilt from immutable history only under a separately reviewed schema and privilege design.

Resolve both the appropriate historical/as-known view and the current deny/incident overlay before new reliance. No largest-timestamp-wins rule, ambiguous fork or backdated restoration may hide a revoked/compromised period. Unknown compromise start denies affected reliance until independently bounded. Recovery needs new approved inventory/verification and evidence; an operator cannot extend dates or edit an old passed result.

A future consumer must recheck binding, config, incident and source-revision heads under an approved transaction/locking or serialization contract immediately before committing its nonfinancial result. A revision change requires rollback/re-resolution; it cannot be ignored because a cached assertion was once usable. Lock ordering with reservations and source domains must be reviewed across all participating writers. This proposal selects no new global lock order and does not claim a local revision fence solves external-provider freshness.

An assertion revoked after an allocation commit does not erase that allocation or authorize a replacement operation. Append contrary evidence, deny new dependent operations and preserve any existing unknown provider obligation for its original reconciliation workflow. Never turn binding invalidation into credit release, consumption, invoice correction, compensation or a second attempt. Historical audit may show a prior as-known result, but cannot use it to bypass a current deny condition.
